Where your Bunker may send, and how fast

Trusted addresses receive withdrawals at once. Everything else waits, and while it waits you can cancel it with this recovery kit. That wait is the only thing that stops someone who steals your day key.

Choose wallets whose keys you hold and that a thief on your everyday computer could not also empty: a hardware wallet is the right answer. Not the wallet you connect to websites. Be careful with an exchange deposit address: someone who steals your day key can send anything in this Bunker there at once, in any amount, and an exchange may not credit a token it does not list or an amount below its minimum. Check every character; these cannot be changed for this Bunker.

Create the kit, then prove it saved

    Your Bunker’s address

    1. Recovery kit. Move it off this device: a USB stick in a drawer, and a second copy somewhere else. Never upload it anywhere.
    2. Day key. This is what you open your Bunker with on the website.
    3. Creation request. Public. Upload it on the website’s Recovery page to build the Bunker, then deposit.
    4. Cancel file. Your emergency stop. If a withdrawal you did not make is waiting, upload this file on the website’s Recovery page: it cancels the withdrawal and kills this day key, at once, without this kit. Keep it where you can reach it in minutes, somewhere other than beside the day key: your phone, your email to yourself. It holds no secret and cannot move anything. Anyone who has it can only do the same thing: stop your Bunker until you come back here with the kit to make a new day key.

    Install new keys.

    A recovery packet replaces every key, kills every earlier day key and cancels a withdrawal that is still waiting. It never moves assets. Making the same packet twice gives the same file, so it is safe to repeat. If you already used your cancel file on the website, the keys have been replaced: enter the generation your Bunker shows now and re-issue its day key.

    Not recovering today? A forgotten recovery password is the most likely way to lose a Bunker for good. Every few months, choose your kit above, type its password and press this. It only checks that the kit still opens; it creates nothing and changes nothing.
