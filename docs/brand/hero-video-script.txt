CLAIM BASIS & PRODUCTION NOTES
• Standard keypair wallet addresses are Ed25519 public keys; this does not mean a visible public key is a current private-key compromise. Solana also supports other account addresses, including PDAs.
• The future quantum threat is a preparation issue, not evidence of an observed break of Solana wallet keys. No countdown or claimed attack date is supported here.
• Separate hash-based withdrawal authorization is the proposed Bunker design from the referenced conversation. Implementation, audits, deployment and real-fund readiness were not verified for this kit. Keep the future-tense narration and development status.
• A PDA's lack of a private key does not by itself make a vault secure. Program authorization and implementation determine who can withdraw. The concept does not claim to make Solana consensus, token issuers, bridges or the user's device post-quantum secure.
